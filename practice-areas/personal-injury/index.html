An injury can leave you managing medical expenses, missed work, and questions about insurance. Hudson, Jones, Jaywork & Fisher represents individuals in Delaware personal injury matters involving motor vehicle accidents, unsafe property, and other negligence-related harm. From offices in Dover and Rehoboth Beach, the firm helps clients review the circumstances, available records, and potential avenues for pursuing a claim.

Vehicle Accidents and Insurance Claims

The firm's existing practice includes claims involving cars, trucks, motorcycles, pedestrians, and cyclists. An attorney may review police reports, photographs, medical records, witness information, and insurance coverage to understand what happened and the parties involved.

Insurance questions may also arise when the other driver is uninsured or has limited coverage. The firm assists with uninsured and underinsured motorist claims, including review of available policies and discussions with insurers. The options depend on the circumstances and the coverage that applies.

Unsafe Property and Other Injury Matters

Injuries can also arise from unsafe property conditions, animal attacks, defective products, and other negligence-related events. The firm reviews the facts and supporting documents to evaluate the matter. Its existing personal injury practice also includes wrongful death claims.

Depending on the facts, a claim may involve medical expenses, lost wages, out-of-pocket costs, pain and suffering, or other losses connected to the injury. The nature and extent of any potential recovery depend on the individual matter.

Preparing for an Initial Discussion

You do not need to have every record organized before contacting the firm. If available, the following information can help explain your situation:

  • A short timeline describing when, where, and how the incident occurred.
  • Photographs, incident or police reports, and witness contact information.
  • Relevant insurance policies, claim numbers, and correspondence from insurers.
  • Medical bills and records, receipts, and information about time missed from work.
  • Any notices, court papers, or response dates you have received.

Contact the firm to discuss the matter and how to provide the relevant documents. Tell the firm about any pending hearing or response date.

Delaware Personal Injury Questions

What kinds of injury matters does the firm handle?

The firm's existing personal injury practice includes motor vehicle accidents, unsafe property conditions, uninsured and underinsured motorist claims, animal attacks, defective products, and wrongful death matters. Contact the firm to discuss whether it can assist with your particular circumstances.

What information is useful when I first contact the firm?

A short account of the incident, the parties involved, insurance information, and any notices or court papers can help start the discussion. Reports, photographs, medical records, bills, and information about missed work may also be useful if available.

Can the firm review an uninsured or underinsured motorist claim?

Yes. The existing practice includes reviewing available insurance coverage and assisting with claims involving uninsured or underinsured motorists. The options depend on the policies and facts involved.

What losses may be considered in an injury claim?

Depending on the matter, relevant losses may include medical expenses, lost wages, out-of-pocket costs, pain and suffering, and other losses tied to the injury. The available remedies and potential recovery depend on the facts.
